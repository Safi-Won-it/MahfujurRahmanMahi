Undergraduate Project • ME 366

Low-Cost Wooden-Frame FDM 3D Printer Fabrication & Recycled PET Filament Characterisation

Faced with commercial printer costs and Bangladesh's plastic waste crisis, we engineered a complete low-cost solution from scratch: a fully wooden-frame Cartesian FDM printer built from locally available materials, paired with a custom bottle cutter and filament extrusion system that transforms waste PET bottles into usable 3D printing filament — a first in South Asian study context. Our hand-extruded recycled PET filament achieved 94.74 N/mm² tensile strength (2.26× stronger than commercial PLA), proving waste plastic can outperform industrial materials.

Undergraduate Project • ME 310

Shell & Tube Heat Exchanger with Helical Inner Tube and Twisted Baffle

Designed and CFD-analyzed a shell-and-tube heat exchanger with a helically coiled inner tube to passively induce Dean-flow secondary vortices. Achieved an 87.5% enhancement in tube-side heat transfer coefficient through helical curvature. Conjugate heat transfer simulations in ANSYS Fluent identified shell-side thermal resistance as the primary bottleneck (94.1%), pointing toward baffle optimization for up to 8× potential total performance gain. A physical prototype was fabricated and successfully pressure-tested at 200 psi.

Undergraduate Project • ME 352

Design and Structural Analysis of a Crane Hook with Shank Using ANSYS

Addressed heavy load lifting safety by designing a crane hook with shank using Winkler-Bach curved beam theory to calculate direct, bending, and combined stresses under rated loads. Validated theoretical models through static structural FEA in ANSYS Workbench, verifying stress distribution, maximum deformation, and factor of safety compliance.
